RentFlow

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

RentFlow ("we", "our", or "us") is a rent tracking application for landlords and tenants in Kenya. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Your full name and email address (provided during sign-up or via Google sign-in)
  • Your role — landlord or tenant

Rental information

  • Building names and locations (entered by landlords)
  • Unit names and monthly rent amounts
  • Payment records including month, amount, status, and M-Pesa reference numbers

Messages

  • Messages sent between landlords and tenants via in-app chat
  • Messages sent in building group chats

Device information

  • Device push notification token (for sending rent reminders — stored only if you grant notification permission)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the rent tracking service — recording and displaying payment history
  • To send push notifications for rent reminders and payment confirmations
  • To enable in-app messaging between landlords and tenants
  • To identify you to other users in the same building (your name is visible to your landlord and fellow tenants in group chat)

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your data is shared only with:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider. Data is stored on secure cloud servers. See supabase.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase — used to deliver push notifications to your device. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Your landlord can see your name and payment records for your unit. Tenants in the same building can see your name in group chat.

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. Payment records remain on file even after a tenant leaves a unit, as they form part of the landlord's records. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

5. Your Rights

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent for push notifications at any time via your device settings

6. Children's Privacy

RentFlow is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
